Project Overview

This proposal outlines the construction of a custom retaining wall designed to stabilize the slope and safely contain structural backfill between the primary cabin and the adjacent deck structure. The wall will span approximately 31 feet, 3 inches.

Structural Code Compliance & Engineering Exemption:

To ensure strict compliance with North Carolina building codes without triggering the requirement for stamped engineering plans, this structure is designed to limit the "unbalanced backfill" (the total retained earth measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the upper dirt grade) to a strict maximum of 47 inches.

Pursuant to the 2024 North Carolina Residential Code (Section R404.4), an engineer is legally required for masonry walls supporting more than 48 inches of unbalanced backfill. By capping the fill at 47 inches, this design strictly bypasses the engineer requirement.

Scope of Work (Structural Wall & Drainage Only)

  • Site Preparation & Utility Management: Machine excavation for bulk trenching, supplemented by precision hand-excavation around the existing electrical meter pole, surface conduit, and PVC plumbing lines to establish a stepped foundation trench. Protective PVC sleeving will be installed for any utility lines passing directly through the wall structure.
  • Foundation & Structural Build: Compacting a 6-inch base of #57 crushed stone and constructing a stepped retaining wall. The final dirt grade on both the high and low sides of the wall will be specifically managed and documented to ensure the retained fill does not exceed 47 inches.
  • Integrated Drainage System: Lining the hillside trench with professional-grade, non-woven geotextile filter fabric. Installation of a 4-inch perforated corrugated drainage pipe embedded in the gravel base, day-lighted away from the cabin structure.
  • Structural Backfill (Gravel Only): Backfilling the immediate 12 inches behind the retaining wall with clean #57 drainage gravel to eliminate hydrostatic pressure. *(For concrete block walls, this includes the installation of structural geogrid tie-backs locked into the hillside).*
  • EXCLUSIONS - NO GENERAL BACKFILLING: General yard backfilling, importing fill dirt or topsoil, grading, and final leveling of the contained yard area up to the existing deck are explicitly excluded from this scope. This bid covers the structural wall and drainage only.

Project Conditions, Terms & Warranty

Structural Code & Liability

  • Code Adherence: By capping the unbalanced structural backfill at a strict maximum of 47 inches, this project remains compliant with North Carolina building codes without requiring stamped engineering plans.
  • Client Liability: If the homeowner elects to alter the grading plan to exceed 47 inches of unbalanced backfill, the homeowner assumes full responsibility for acquiring and funding all necessary engineering stamped plans and Cherokee County building permits prior to the commencement of construction.

Project Parameters

  • Hidden Damage: Proposal assumes existing sub-surface conditions and utilities match the provided descriptions. Unforeseen rock excavation or unknown utility conflicts will be bid separately via change order if discovered.
  • Proposal Expiration: All values listed in this proposal are guaranteed for 30 calendar days from the date of issue.
